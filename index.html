Privacy Policy

Tallywise: Pill Counter · Last updated November 11, 2026

Tallywise counts pills in photos and keeps an optional log of counts. This policy explains what the app does with information.

What we collect

Nothing. Tallywise has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs. We do not receive any of your data.

Camera and photos

The camera and the photos you pick are used only to count pills. Counting runs entirely on your iPhone; images are never uploaded. A photo is stored only when you save a count to the log, and it stays in the app's storage on your device.

Your count log

Medication names, amounts, initials and notes you enter are stored only on your iPhone. They leave the device only when you choose to share a photo, a PDF or a CSV file yourself. Deleting a count or the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your payment details.

Children

Tallywise is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Support

Questions, feature ideas or a count that came out wrong? Open an issue at github.com/marmeq/tallywise/issues.

Tips for accurate counts

  • Use a background that contrasts with the pills: a dark tray for white pills, a light tray for dark ones.
  • Spread the pills in one layer. Touching is fine, stacked is not.
  • Avoid glare and the shadow of your hand. Hold the phone straight above the tray.
  • Check the dots: tap a missed pill to add it, tap a dot to remove it. Raise the sensitivity for tiny or faint pills.

Tallywise is a counting aid and does not identify medications or give medical advice. Always verify counts of controlled medications as your workplace or pharmacist requires.
